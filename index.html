The Flywheel

How KAAS works

One loop connecting clinical design, industrial kitchen execution, and measurable client outcomes.

1

Experts Design

Dieticians & coaches build macro-precise menus in the provider dashboard — per client, per goal, per condition.

2

Kitchens Execute

Central cloud kitchens receive the day's orders, cook to spec, and portion with gram-level macro accuracy.

3

Clients Track

Meals arrive with QR-coded nutrition cards. Clients log adherence, hunger, energy & weight in their dashboard.

4

Data Loops Back

Adherence & outcome data flows to the provider, who tunes the menu. Compliance becomes automatic, not willpower.

Roadmap

From meal delivery to health infrastructure

Phase 1 · MVP — Menu → Kitchen → Doorstep

Provider dashboard for menu design & client management. Client app for meal tracking & goals. One pilot cloud kitchen, daily delivery routes, QR-coded nutrition cards.

Phase 2 · Vitals & Diagnosis Data

Integrate wearables and lab reports. Pre-built meal templates for diabetes, hypertension, PCOS, CKD. Providers see HbA1c, BP, lipid trends alongside adherence data.

Phase 3 · Adaptive Nutrition Engine

AI adjusts menus automatically based on outcomes: stalled weight, glucose spikes, low energy flags trigger recipe swaps — approved by the human provider before the kitchen fires.
