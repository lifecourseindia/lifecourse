The Flywheel

How KAAS works

Every stakeholder gets software; KAAS owns the food layer between prescription and plate.

1

Experts Design

Dieticians & coaches build macro-precise, condition-aware meal plans per client in the provider dashboard.

2

Kitchens Execute

Cloud kitchens receive orders as KOT tickets, cook to spec, and portion with ±5g accuracy. No demand-generation cost.

3

Clients Track

Meals, macros, weight, glucose, BP and energy — logged in one client dashboard. Adherence becomes visible, not self-reported.

4

Data Loops Back

Vitals + adherence flow to the provider, who tunes the plan. Outcomes compound → retention compounds → revenue compounds.

Business Model

Kaas monetizes every layer of the food-health stack

25–30%

1. Meal margin (B2B2C)

Kitchen sells prescription meals at a premium over cost. Providers are the demand engine — CAC for food drops ~60% vs. consumer D2C.

SaaS ₹999/mo

2. Provider software

Dietician console: client management, plan builder, vitals tracking, outcome analytics. Free up to 10 clients, then tiered SaaS + % of meal revenue.

8% take-rate

3. Kitchen marketplace

Partner cloud kitchens plug into KAAS demand. We take 8% of order value; they fill idle capacity with pre-specified, zero-CAC orders.

Phase 2+

4. Data & diagnostics

De-identified adherence-outcome datasets for insurers & employers; condition-care programs (diabetes, CKD) sold as covered benefits.
