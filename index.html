BEHAVIOUR DIAGNOSIS

Awareness × Expression

Knowing a problem exists does not automatically mean the person is ready or willing to act.

LATENT
APPARENT / EXPRESSED
UNAWARE
Unaware + Latent The person does not recognise the problem and does not actively express a need.
Unaware + Apparent The behaviour or health problem is visible to others even though the person may not recognise it.
AWARE
Aware + Latent The person knows the problem exists but does not yet translate awareness into action.
Aware + Apparent The problem is recognised and the need for change is clearly expressed.

BEHAVIOUR DIAGNOSTIC ENGINE

COM-B

Before designing an intervention, diagnose what is actually preventing the behaviour.

C

Capability

Does the person have the physical and psychological capability, knowledge and skills required?

O

Opportunity

Does the physical and social environment make the behaviour possible, accessible and socially supported?

M

Motivation

Do automatic and reflective processes create enough motivation to perform the behaviour?

INTERACTIVE DIAGNOSTIC

What is blocking the behaviour?

Use this quick diagnostic to identify the dominant behavioural barrier.

1. Does the person know what to do?

2. Do they have the skills/resources?

3. Does the environment support the behaviour?

4. Do they genuinely want to change?

5. Is there a reliable prompt or cue?

BEHAVIOUR DESIGN

Principles for designing health behaviour

Move from telling people what they should do toward designing conditions in which behaviour can happen.

Reduce friction Make the desired behaviour easier, shorter and more accessible.
Make it visible Use cues, salience and environmental prompts at the moment of decision.
Build capability Teach skills and create confidence, not just awareness.
Use social proof People interpret behaviour partly through what they see others doing.
Design for identity Connect behaviour with the person's sense of who they are.
Create immediate feedback Help people see progress before distant health outcomes arrive.
Start small Reduce the initial behavioural barrier so the first action becomes achievable.
Design the environment Change defaults, availability and context where behaviour actually occurs.
