Commercialization workflow

Move beyond listing: define the need, find the solution, validate the value proposition and facilitate the right commercial path.

1

Define

Translate a market or technology problem into measurable search criteria.

2

Scout & Filter

Search universities, startups and adjacent technologies; remove weak or duplicate leads.

3

Validate

Compare TRL, IP, performance, cost, market application and commercialization readiness.

4

Engage & Deal

Facilitate inventor/TTO introductions, NDA, evaluation, licensing, startup or partnership discussions.
